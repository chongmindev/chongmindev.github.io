A groundwater analytics system that turns raw well measurements into interpretable trend signals: each site is classified Improving, Stable, or Declining by comparing conditions before and after SGMA with a nonparametric significance test, then mapped across California for local planning and policy context.

Omnibowl In progress

An NLP pipeline that turns a textbook PDF into pyramidal quizbowl tossups, so a class can be studied in the style of protobowl. It discovers each book's layout into a profile, parses sentences with spaCy, ranks answer terms by corpus keyness, and links mentions with a local coreference model; every clue traces back to the textbook sentence it came from. Tossups play solo or in multiplayer rooms over FastAPI WebSockets.

Repo

Cube Recon In progress

Recovering a speedcube's starting scramble from video of the solve, using as little footage as possible. The plan: computer vision to track the cube and read its stickers, a sequence model trained with CTC loss to transcribe the moves, and Bayesian state tracking plus constraint solving (Z3) to carry every observation back to the first frame. Where a clip can't pin down every piece, it reports a distribution over legal states instead of one guess. Ground truth comes from smart cube move logs synced to the video.

Thumbs Up Coffee Explore In progress

An iOS app (Expo) for rating independent specialty cafes in Seoul's Apgujeong and Sinsa. You check in, give a cafe one to three thumbs, then place it head to head against cafes you've already ranked, building a personal ranked list instead of star averages. Seeded with 270 audited independent coffee shops from the Kakao Local API, with franchises filtered out.
