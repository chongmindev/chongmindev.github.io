Omnibowl In progress

An NLP pipeline that turns a textbook PDF into pyramidal quizbowl tossups, so a class can be studied in the style of protobowl. It discovers each book's layout into a profile, parses sentences with spaCy, ranks answer terms by corpus keyness, and links mentions with a local coreference model; every clue traces back to the textbook sentence it came from. Tossups play solo or in multiplayer rooms over FastAPI WebSockets.
